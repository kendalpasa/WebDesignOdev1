Do you know why a frog is carrying wood to a fire? I don't either. But to survive, it must keep bringing wood to the flames.

With 3 different upgrades, you can:

  • With the Speed upgrade: Move faster
  • With the Strong Log upgrade: Make the logs fuel the fire more
  • With the Capacity upgrade: Carry more wood at once

You need to keep the frog alive for as long as you can. 

How to Play:

  • WASD to move
  • Touch a log to pick it up
  • Carry the logs to the fire
  • Every log fuels the flames

Good luck!
